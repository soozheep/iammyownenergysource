# I Am My Own Energy Source

Live at: https://iammyownenergysource.vercel.app

## Assets to upload

Upload these files from your Google Drive folder **10 I Am My Own Energy Source** into the corresponding folders:

### Root of `assets/`
- `iamoes-cover.PNG` (cover art)
- `10_I Am My Own Energy Source.mp3` (or rename the mastered MP3 to this)

### `assets/lyrics/`
- `iamoes-01.png` through `iamoes-11.png` (the 11 lyric cards)

### `assets/cards/`
- `iamoes-card-01.png` through `iamoes-card-04.png` (the 4 teaching/pattern cards)

After uploading, the page will display everything correctly. Vercel will auto-deploy on push.
